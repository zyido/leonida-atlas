```bash
python3 - <<'PY'

```

## The region aggregates — `data/regions.json`

Generated by the atlas on 2026-11-18T05:57:22.576Z from the same catalogue. It
carries the six region definitions, their alias lists, the published totals and
the field definitions the audit scripts follow. Region assignment is done on
the final address label only — there is no coordinate-based inference.
